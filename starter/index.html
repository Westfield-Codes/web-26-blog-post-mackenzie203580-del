Teen Book Reviews

Posted on October 22, 2026 by Mackenzie St.Peter
All Posts

From being trapped and ready to fight to the death, all while finding your starcrossed lover knowing only one can live, this month's book is The Hunger Games! Every month, I will pick one or two books to review. In the comments section you can recommend to me some of your favorite books and if they’re good I might choose to review it. All my books are clean and teen friendly, I hope you enjoy them!

The Hunger Games by Suzzanne Collins is set in a dystopian future, where as punishment for war each of the divided “districts” each send one male and one female tribute to fight to the death televised in an arena. Two teens from the most "unsuccessful" district face the odds of the arena. Brave teen Katniss Everdeen volunteers in place of her younger sister, she and Peeta Melark prepare to face the arena knowing only one can return. Against all odds these starcrossed lovers try to survive.

Things the book has:
  • A romantic plotline
  • Two starcross lovers
  • Two boys liking the same girl
  • A girl who faces the end of the world, with the lover who must die
Bad things the book has:
  • Death
  • Communism
  • Child abuse by the goverment
  • Poverty
  • Government borders
  • Gangs (the career’s)
I really liked this book, especially because of the way that even during a disaster, these teens don't throw away their values and they stay true to themselfs. The main character Katniss relates to me because she would do anything to help her family just like me. I differ from her because I don't think I could be as brave as her during at time like this. If you like dystopian, action, and romance books, then you will definitely enjoy this book.

The Movie

The book series has been adapted into 5 different movies so far, with a 6th on the way. The movie leaves out some key detail, and adds some parts to the plot that weren't originaly there. The movie completely leaves out Petta's leg injury, and Katnisses hearing loss. In the book, the character Madge gives Katniss her iconic mockingjay pin, but the movie leaves out the entier plot line of this.
